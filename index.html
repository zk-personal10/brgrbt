02 — The idea

The burger is the wordmark.

Most food brands park an icon next to a name. Burger Bite doesn’t. The bun is a letter. The filling is the word. Read it once and you’ve already ordered.

  1. Read it top to bottom.Four stripes. One object.
  2. The bun is the letter.A B is carved into the top bun. You read it before you see it.
  3. The filling is the name.URGER sits in melted cheese between lettuce and tomato. The word is the patty.
  4. The base closes the stack.A second B in the bottom bun, ITE in cream. BITE finishes the burger.
  5. One object.Not an icon plus a wordmark. The burger is the wordmark.

07 — Why it works

Seven reasons it sticks.

01
0.0s

Instant recognition

Category and name register together, before anyone consciously reads a letter.

02

Simple silhouette

Fill it solid and it’s still a burger. A shape you can draw from memory.

03

Food is the type

The letters are cheese and bread. There is nothing to separate or lose.

04

Strong colour memory

Orange, yellow, green, red. Five hues that read “burger” with the logo covered.

05
Built to ruin white shirts.

A voice with nerve

Confident, self-aware and funny without trying too hard. It admits the mess.

06

Works small or large

Thick outlines and rounded forms survive from billboard down to favicon.

07

Readable from a distance

Blur it hard. The blob is still orange on top, green in the middle. Still a burger.
