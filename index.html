02 — The idea

The burger is the wordmark.

Most food brands park an icon next to a name. Burger Bite doesn’t. The bun is a letter. The filling is the word. Read it once and you’ve already ordered.

  1. Read it top to bottom.Four stripes. One object.
  2. The bun is the letter.A B is carved into the top bun. You read it before you see it.
  3. The filling is the name.URGER sits in melted cheese between lettuce and tomato. The word is the patty.
  4. The base closes the stack.A second B in the bottom bun, ITE in cream. BITE finishes the burger.
  5. One object.Not an icon plus a wordmark. The burger is the wordmark.
